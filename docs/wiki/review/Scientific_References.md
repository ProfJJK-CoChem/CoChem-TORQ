# TORQ reference verification and publication-readiness ledger

This is a targeted primary-source audit, not a completed systematic literature review. Retrieval used the environment's normal HTTPS proxy and TLS verification. GitHub source files below were re-fetched at immutable commits and compared byte-for-byte with the initially inspected files. [source retrieval manifest](sources/metadata.json) records URLs, revisions, SHA-256 digests and retrieval failures; [upstream revisions](sources/heads.json) records observed upstream heads. No scientific engine was executed during this audit. The upstream heads are evidence snapshots, **not** proposed production dependency pins.

## Scientific corrections supported by these checks

1. A DSD-family model has the schematic form `E = E_one-electron+NN+J + a_x E_x^HF + (1-a_x)E_x^DFA + a_c E_c^DFA + c_OS E_c,OS^PT2 + c_SS E_c,SS^PT2 + E_disp`. This is a template, not a numerical recipe: orbital-generation parameters, restricted/unrestricted definitions, density fitting, frozen-core treatment, basis and dispersion parameters must be explicit. Nuclear derivatives are derivatives of the full method, including orbital response. Combining unrelated DFT and MP2 gradient objects by scalar arithmetic does not establish that derivative. Validate any implementation against an independent reference and finite differences of its own energy; do not declare open-source revDSD geometry optimization production-ready from an energy formula.
2. Distinguish analytic harmonic Hessians from finite differences of Hessians used to construct cubic/semidiagonal quartic force fields, and distinguish both from the VPT2 perturbative approximation. Remove phrases such as “exact anharmonic force fields” or “analytic CCSD(T) VPT2” unless a precise derivative-level implementation and domain are documented. CFOUR release/method/reference capabilities remain a certification item.
3. All benchmark numbers must retain the precise observable, dataset, uncertainty statistic, protocol, engine/version, and hardware. Library documentation, one successful example, or a DOI alone does not establish a universal accuracy or runtime bound.

## Unverified, incomplete, or suspect citations in the supplied documents

- `CoChem-TORQ_True_SRS_Iterated.md`, line 31, cites `10.26434/chemrxiv-2024-v1234`. This is placeholder-like and was not verified. Remove it from normative support until the author supplies a resolvable bibliographic record; do not represent it as established evidence.
- The same document, line 29, labels a Bunker reference “2010” beside DOI `10.1002/bscb.19800890712`; this is an apparent year/record mismatch requiring metadata verification. A DOI's embedded digits alone are insufficient to reconstruct a replacement citation.
- Many iterative suggestions give only an author and year or an incompletely specified paper (for example “Barone et al., J. Chem. Phys. 2013”), or attach a general method paper to a new exact threshold such as `sigma_E < 0.15 kcal/mol`. These citations do not establish that TORQ-specific threshold or claimed coverage. Record such numbers as hypotheses/configuration defaults with calibration experiments, not literature facts.
- “Ensemble disagreement directly reflects epistemic model error,” guaranteed global-minimum retention after fixed energy cutoffs, and universal assignment/accuracy guarantees exceed the evidence recorded in the documents. Require domain-specific calibration and false-negative/coverage measurements.
- “SWEBOK,” “OSI,” “W3C PROV-O,” and the unreferenced phrase “Zero-Mock verification protocols” do not validate the proposed scientific equations, license-token names, or cryptographic design. Cite actual standards sections or label a rule as a TORQ design decision.
- Method Matrix §5.8's Guo et al., *JCP* **148**, 011101 (2018), DOI `10.1063/1.5011798`, and the water-dimer attribution to Plumley/Dannenberg require source-level checking for method/engine/basis correspondence. This audit did **not** verify those article texts. Treat suspected MPQC/ORCA or F12 substitutions as unresolved source conflicts, not independently proved bibliographic fraud.
- The matrix's 319-item link list is an index, not a verified publication bibliography. Software documentation, vendor pages, benchmarks, papers, preprints and third-party posts should have distinct evidence classes. Publication bibliography entries need complete authors/title/venue/year/DOI or URL, an accessed date for changing documentation, and a claim-to-source locator.

## Access limits and outstanding validation

The normal proxy denied access to the CFOUR official manual at `https://cfour.uni-mainz.de/cfour/index.php?n=Main.AnalyticEnergyDerivatives` (HTTP tunnel 403). SourceForge file-download redirects were also denied; the independently accessible JANPA project Wiki supplied the needed capability evidence. No proxy, TLS or policy bypass was used. Other publisher articles named above were not fetched merely to repeat already known network restrictions.

Before describing the complete program as scientifically validated or deployable: obtain the relevant released engine manuals/license terms, certify engine-method-derivative tuples on real calculations, review the identified scientific papers and their supporting data, validate every export against its consumer, and run the benchmark/uncertainty plan. These are validation prerequisites, not claims fulfilled by this document revision.
