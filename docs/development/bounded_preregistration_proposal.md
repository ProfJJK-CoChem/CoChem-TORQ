This is a proposed observational use-case, not a statement about the user's actual instrument. Only authentic measured line positions are eligible. Catalogue predictions are separate objects. ## Targets that follow an observable use-case

A proposed **100 kHz absolute line-frequency resolution** provides one concrete high-accuracy identification use-case. It must be confirmed or replaced before registration using the actual channel width, instrumental calibration, fitted line-centre uncertainty, Doppler/pressure corrections, linewidth and spectral-confusion setting. It is an engineering objective, not a published universal revDSD accuracy claim or an assertion of achievable de novo accuracy. For scale, 100 kHz at 100 GHz corresponds to about 0.30 km/s through c × delta_nu/nu; an astronomical application must separately fix its velocity and rest-frequency conventions.

Under this candidate use-case:

- Require a confirmed experimental standard uncertainty no greater than **10 kHz** for a measured line entering the physical pass/fail gate. This reserves a factor of ten below the proposed resolution. Unknown uncertainty remains null; the parentheses printed in the secondary B0 table are not automatically one-sigma errors.
- Propose maximum line change no greater than **1 kHz** under each final numerical tightening. This is a 1% numerical budget allocation, not molecular predictive uncertainty. Tightening convergence controls, grids and derivative stencils must be distinguished from changing basis or physical theory.
- Propose maximum absolute prediction-minus-measurement residual no greater than **100 kHz for every frozen selected line**. Report mean, RMS and tails alongside that worst-case criterion. The measurement uncertainty and genuine numerical variation stay separate from model discrepancy; do not subtract them to create a calibrated theory confidence interval.
- Derive per-axis A/B/C and distortion-parameter engineering limits from the qualified selected Hamiltonian's sensitivities, delta_nu ≈ J delta_p, and its full parameter covariance when genuinely supplied. Fix the allocations before testing. There is no defensible universal A/B/C percentage that guarantees line accuracy over different bands/J/Ka.
- Treat correct frequency as necessary evidence for this use-case, not sufficient identification. A later real-spectrum gate must predefine competitor species, line blends, assignments, at least the actual detectable transitions, intensities/populations and false-positive control. No universal "three lines proves identity" rule is adopted.

The exact numerical **1e-10 relative well-conditioned inertia-kernel check** comes from SRS V-ROT and remains a same-masses/same-geometry software criterion. It does not qualify experimental B0. Historical Psi4/PySCF B2PLYP output cannot receive an invented energy tolerance: density fitting, quadrature, basis primitives and historical engine version differ or are unavailable. Display its genuine residual. For a future matched numerical component gate, first verify the full model and input conventions, measure independent solver/grid convergence and bound reference printing/rounding. Then prospectively freeze a tolerance reflecting those measured numerical contributions. Unknown method provenance blocks a same-model acceptance claim.

No intensity threshold is supplied because authenticated line intensities, temperatures, instrument response, nuclear-spin populations and uncertainty are absent. Catalogue intensities derived from calculated dipoles remain theoretical. No discovery search window is guessed: its width must be fixed from development errors and an actual spectral-density/competitor study before the held-out challenge.

## Real method and nuclear-motion contracts

The method matrix remains binding. A recipe freezes the electronic equation and orbital-generating GKS functional; basis primitive/contraction bytes and auxiliary basis; spin/reference/frozen core; relativistic treatment; complete dispersion kernel/charge model/ATM definition and response; engine/source/container versions; isotope masses and physical constants release; integral/grid/SCF conventions and stability diagnostics. The primary original revDSD-D4 orbital/final-energy definition is currently unresolved. The acquired primary D3 sample and separately official D4 parameters cannot be stitched into an accepted exact D4 electronic model.

Qualify energy, full orbital response and higher derivatives as separate genuine milestones. Compare independent matched finite differences at multiple actual step sizes, test translation/rotation covariance and follow the same electronic stationary solution. Record truncation, cancellation and solver error. A response-incomplete sum or an imported vendor method name does not qualify exact gradients.

Freeze coordinates/Eckart frame, mass substitution, mode labels/degeneracy, displacement stencil/step sizes, actual cubic and semi-diagonal quartic force field, resonance thresholds/diagonalization, and Watson reduction/axis/distortion/hyperfine conventions. Use delta_B_vib = B0 - Be. Only an actual qualified force field and nuclear-motion result supplies the correction. A harmonic Hessian is not a general VPT2 correction. Semi-experimental Be_SE = B0_exp - delta_B_vib_calc is a separately labelled **anchored Product B** and cannot be scored as de novo Product A. General fitted B0/constants and bounded nonresonant model B0 are distinct quantities requiring a justified convention mapping.

When a property is missing, ARCH-02 directs the agent to plan and execute the identifiable additional genuine calculation with its method, source, cost and typed provenance. Preserve valid earlier stages. The current water baseline supplies Be and harmonic frequencies; **there is no B0 value to compare to experimental B0**, and harmonic frequencies must not be compared as measured fundamental bands.

## Blinding, scores and finite-sample limits

Freeze sources, source-dependence grouping, domain, candidate order, inclusion/diagnostic thresholds, exact recipes, targets and budgets before any prospective held-out scoring. A separate curation process can inspect reference bytes; calculation/model tuning cannot inspect held-out residuals. Seal prediction and original native-artifact digests before scoring. A seal is not enforced information isolation; disclose any actual exposure. An inspected family becomes development in a versioned future campaign, while the original trial outcome remains retained.

Report all per-datum signed/absolute errors, equal-parent and equal-family summaries, worst line errors, band/J/Ka/state coverage, intended/executed/failed/blocked/excluded counts and actual cost. Individual axes or dozens of lines do not create independent molecular samples. Missing required observables remain in intended-case denominators and never contribute a zero residual or false pass. Exclusions may use only frozen reference/identity/applicability conditions, never the error obtained.

For candidate nominal **95%** family-group split conformal coverage, use k = ceil((n+1) × 0.95) and the exact kth group score. At least **19 independent exchangeable calibration groups** are needed for a finite order statistic. This is only a mathematical minimum; it establishes neither a trustworthy interval nor chemical transfer nor scientific 95% coverage. The proposed initial campaign has at most **one calibration family and two held-out families**, fewer after source merging. Consequently its finite 95% interval is unavailable, and it reports descriptive errors with no calibrated uncertainty claim. Changing the nominal level after seeing errors to obtain a finite interval is prohibited.

Exchangeability between carbon-fluoride calibration and nitrogen-oxide/heterofluoride testing is not established. A later uncertainty campaign needs a defined sampling population and actual independent groups or a justified explicit alternative inferential model. To illustrate empirical scale only, even **59 independent predeclared held-out groups all covered** yield a one-sided 95% exact binomial lower coverage bound of about **0.9505**, under iid/exchangeable Bernoulli assumptions. That calculation is neither a sufficiency rule nor a claim 59 suitable independent groups exist. Source dependence, selection or transfer breaks its interpretation; finite conformal calibration and empirical coverage certification are separate problems.

## Resources that can be planned honestly

The repository teaching proposal has unaccepted maxima of 64 jobs, two cores/job, 4096 MB, 1800 seconds/job and 230400 CPU-seconds total. They are proposal fields, not an allocated GitHub allowance or a budget for advanced derivatives. Current examples have genuine declared bounded execution limits: water 120 seconds/one core/1024 MB; NH3 component helper 300 seconds/one affinity core/1024 MiB sampled worker RSS and 256 MiB minimum free scratch. One earlier genuine NH3 attempt took 86.067 seconds and peaked at 782512128 sampled RSS bytes. These are observations/limits for those cases, not runtime forecasts for arbitrary molecules, derivatives or hosted machines.

For a Hessian-based cubic/semi-diagonal-quartic convergence pilot, let m=3N-6 for nonlinear species (3N-5 for linear) and d=3N. Central displacements at h and h/2 require approximately 1+4m Cartesian Hessian calls when a common equilibrium is cached and the normal-coordinate formulation is validated. If each Hessian requires 1+2d finite-difference gradients, and each gradient in turn requires 1+2d energies, a conservative uncached planning bound is (1+4m) × (1+2d)^2 primitive energy calls: **4693 for water and 15625 for formaldehyde**. These are algebraic planning examples, not executed counts. They exclude geometry optimization, independent derivative checks, resonance/property derivatives, failures, extra basis/core/relativistic contributions and a complete arbitrary quartic tensor. Caching, symmetry and analytic derivatives change the actual DAG; no implementation is presumed.

The next agent cost pilot must measure actual energy/gradient/Hessian walltime, CPU, RSS, scratch and failure rates, then compile an immutable finite DAG and maximum primitive-call/CPU/walltime/storage budget for each profile. Higher-level derivatives cannot be promised inside the teaching proposal based on a fast energy example. Budget exhaustion keeps the real completed stages and reports incomplete. Canonical calculations remain qualified GitHub Actions worker images and the interface remains Codespaces; local cloud evidence does not certify student deployment.
